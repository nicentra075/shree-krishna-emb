This Privacy Policy describes how Shree Krishna Embroidery ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Shree Krishna Embroidery mobile application and related services.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and profile photo you provide when signing up (via email/password, phone OTP, Google, or Apple sign-in).
  • Purchase information: your orders, invoices, and the digital designs you have purchased. Payments are processed by Razorpay; we do not store your card, UPI, or banking credentials.
  • Device tokens: a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can send you order updates and announcements — controllable in Notification Preferences.
  • Usage data: designs you view and save (wishlist, recently viewed) to personalise your experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and deliver purchased design files.
  • To process payments and generate invoices (via Razorpay).
  • To send transactional and (optional) promotional notifications.
  • To provide customer support and improve the service.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Storage) with role-based security rules. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

We use Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, notifications) and Razorpay (payment processing). Their privacy policies apply to data they process.
